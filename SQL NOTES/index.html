Built-in vs user-defined

Built-in functions come with MySQL. User-defined ones are written with CREATE FUNCTION.

Revision: the confusing pairs

LENGTH vs CHAR_LENGTH

LENGTH counts bytes. CHAR_LENGTH counts characters.

CONCAT vs CONCAT_WS

CONCAT adds no spaces. CONCAT_WS adds your separator between items.

LOCATE vs INSTR

LOCATE(substr, str). INSTR(str, substr). Order is reversed.

ROUND vs TRUNCATE

ROUND looks at the next digit. TRUNCATE just cuts extra digits.

CEIL vs FLOOR

CEIL goes up to the next whole number. FLOOR goes down.

DATEDIFF vs TIMESTAMPDIFF

DATEDIFF gives days only (later date first). TIMESTAMPDIFF gives any unit (unit first).

DATE_FORMAT vs STR_TO_DATE

DATE_FORMAT turns a date into text. STR_TO_DATE turns text into a date.

INSERT()

Replaces characters inside a string. It does not insert a database row.
